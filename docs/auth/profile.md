---
title: Your profile
description: Set a display name and avatar on your GateKeeper account.
---

Your profile page at `/` (the home screen after sign-in) lets you set a display name and pull in a Gravatar avatar.

## Display name

Enter a name in the **Display name** field and click **Save**. This name appears in the admin audit log, the user list, and anywhere your account is shown in the interface.

If no display name is set, your email address is shown instead.

## Avatar

GateKeeper uses [Gravatar](https://gravatar.com) for profile pictures. Gravatar links your picture to your email address. If you have a Gravatar account with the same email you use to sign in, click the refresh icon next to your avatar circle to pull it in.

The image is fetched server-side and cached in the database - your email address is never exposed to Gravatar from the browser.

If no Gravatar exists for your email, your initials are shown instead.

To update your avatar after changing your Gravatar picture, click the refresh icon again.
