# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This is a small open source project. Participate in good faith.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Give and receive feedback constructively
- Focus on the problem, not the person
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions that do not fit the project's direction

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing private information about others without consent

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer via the contact information on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and responded to. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban participants who violate these standards.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum associated with this repository.
